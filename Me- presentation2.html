Concept 4: Safety

Safety: What Do We Warn Patients About?

Common

  • Nausea, vomiting
  • Diarrhea, constipation
  • Abdominal discomfort

Warnings

  • Pancreatitis
  • Gallbladder disease
  • Dehydration/AKI
  • Hypoglycemia

Contraindicated

  • Medullary thyroid carcinoma
  • MEN2

Always review pregnancy plans and peri-procedural considerations.

Summary

Take-Home Messages

GLP-1s are powerful treatments for obesity—not generic anti-aging drugs.

Menopause changes fat distribution more than the scale suggests; protect muscle and bone.

Discuss benefits, side effects, long-term expectations, and weight regain before starting.

Excellent documentation is clinical care.
